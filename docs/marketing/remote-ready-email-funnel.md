# Remote Ready Verification Email Funnel

Manual 10-email lifecycle campaign for high-intent Inglevo leads from `/remote-ready`, blog CTAs and lead magnets.

This campaign is copy-ready for Resend, ConvertKit, Mailchimp, Beehiiv or any ESP. It does not require API, database, automation or product changes.

## Campaign Strategy

- Audience: opted-in leads and existing Inglevo leads who showed interest in Remote Ready, blog content, templates or lead magnets.
- Language: English only.
- Sender voice: Inglevo team.
- Primary conversion: start verification/signup.
- Core promise: help candidates build stronger remote-work signals, English confidence, CV/profile assets and interview readiness.
- Claim boundary: do not promise jobs, salaries, placement, sponsorship, hiring outcomes or guaranteed interviews.

## Manual Follow-Up Logic

Use these rules if the ESP supports tags, manual filters or simple segments.

| Trigger | Timing | Action |
|---|---:|---|
| Clicked CTA but did not sign up | 48 hours after click | Send the short follow-up below. |
| Signed up | Immediately | Remove from this campaign and let product onboarding take over. |
| Never clicked by E05 | After E05 | Continue sequence, but do not add extra pressure emails. |
| Replied to any email | Same business day if possible | Reply personally with one next step and one link back to verification. |
| Unsubscribed | Immediately | Remove from all marketing sends. |

## ESP Setup Checklist

- Create one campaign named `Remote Ready 30D`.
- Add tags or filters for `remote-ready-lead`, `lead-magnet`, `blog-lead`, `clicked-signup`, `signed-up`, `replied`, `unsubscribed`.
- Paste each email with its own subject, preview text and CTA URL.
- Replace `{{first_name}}` with the ESP merge tag. Use `there` or omit the greeting if no first name exists.
- Replace `{{cta_url}}` with the email-specific tracked URL.
- Replace `[Physical mailing address]` and `[unsubscribe link]` before sending.
- Stop the campaign when the person signs up or unsubscribes.
- Do not send this campaign to purchased lists.

## QA Checklist

- Every email has exactly one main CTA.
- Every CTA uses `utm_campaign=remote_ready_30d`.
- Every main sequence email has a unique `utm_content` from `e01` to `e10`.
- The clicked-no-signup follow-up uses `utm_content=clicked_no_signup_followup`.
- No email says or implies: guaranteed employment, guaranteed salary, guaranteed interviews, US placement, sponsorship guarantee or employer access guarantee.
- No subject line uses false urgency, fake personalization or misleading claims.
- Footer includes sender identity, physical mailing address placeholder and unsubscribe placeholder.
- Paragraphs are short enough for mobile reading.
- Product claims match the current Inglevo positioning: role English, CV/profile signals, templates, Job CRM, interview practice, remote readiness and verification profile.

## Metrics To Review

Track the campaign by email number and segment.

| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Open rate | Indicates subject and sender recognition. Use cautiously because open tracking can be unreliable. |
| Click rate | Primary signal of interest in the CTA. |
| Signup starts | Shows whether the CTA and landing page are aligned. |
| Completed signups | Main conversion before Stripe is connected. |
| Replies | Shows message-market fit and objections. |
| Unsubscribes | Detects fatigue or wrong audience targeting. |
| Conversions by `utm_content` | Identifies which email actually moves people. |
