# Inglevo Product Context

Inglevo helps Spanish-speaking professionals practice English for career moments, especially interviews, workplace conversations, and role-specific communication.

## Product intent

- Help users become clearer, calmer, and more fluent in professional English.
- Focus on practical speaking and writing situations instead of generic language lessons.
- Support gradual improvement through practice history, feedback, and level-aware prompts.

## Initial audience

- Spanish-speaking adults preparing for English interviews or professional conversations.
- Users with intermediate goals who need confidence, structure, and repeated practice.

## Product pillars

- Practical: every exercise should map to a real career situation.
- Personal: practice should adapt to level, role, goals, and weak spots.
- Supportive: feedback should be direct, useful, and encouraging.
- Measurable: progress should be visible through sessions, responses, and feedback history.
